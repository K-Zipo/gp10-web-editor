Connect your GP-10

Edit the current patch of a BOSS GP-10 connected over USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge, press Connect, and allow access to MIDI devices. The GP-10 USB driver needs to be installed.

No driver, or on Android? Use Connect over USB. Chrome then talks to the GP-10 directly. This works only while the GP-10 driver is not installed or running.

Select a block to edit it. Double-click or use its power button to switch it on or off. Drag to reorder.
